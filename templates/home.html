INTELLIGENT ARCHITECTURE

How Veritas Uncovers Deception

Sophisticated computational linguistics combined with statistical machine learning algorithms trained across decades of authentic and fabricate media.

NLP Stopword & Noise Removal

Filters out 179 non-discriminative grammatical articles, punctuation, and typographical noise to isolate the semantic core of every news piece.

Porter Stemming Algorithm

Extracts fundamental morphological word roots (e.g. "fabricated", "fabrication" → "fabric") to standardize syntactic vocabulary across publishers.

5,000 TF-IDF N-Grams

Weights vocabulary by Term Frequency × Inverse Document Frequency, penalizing ubiquitous words while amplifying high-leverage deceptive phraseology.

Regularized Logistic Regression

Applies mathematical decision boundaries with L2 regularization to balance high precision and sensitivity without overfitting.

Sub-Millisecond Inference

Optimized vectorized matrix transformations enable lightning-fast predictions suited for high-throughput newsrooms and browser extensions.

Calibrated Forensic Metrics

Generates transparent confidence probabilities, keyword attribution tags, and full audit logs for academic or journalistic scrutiny.

END-TO-END PIPELINE

The Verification Workflow

From raw unstructured text to calibrated probabilistic verdict in five automated stages.

STAGE 01
Text Ingestion

Parses raw input text, handles empty characters, and strips HTML markup or escape artifacts.

STAGE 02
Stopword Filter

Removes 179 standard English stopwords and strips special characters, punctuation, and digits.

STAGE 03
Porter Stemmer

Normalizes morphological variations into singular linguistic stem representations.

STAGE 04
TF-IDF Matrix

Transforms processed tokens into high-dimensional vector representations across 5,000 features.

STAGE 05
Logistic Classifier

Computes sigmoid activation function yielding calibrated confidence scores for Real vs Fake.
